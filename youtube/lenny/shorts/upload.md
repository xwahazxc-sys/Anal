# Lenny's Scam Lab: Shorts (4)

Each Short is vertical 1080×1920, 42–46 s, with captions burned in and Dark Fog music.
Each one ends on "Full video on my channel" and leads to its full episode.

**When uploading each Short:**
- **Related video** (YouTube Studio → the Short → "Related video"): the full episode listed below. This is the main lever: it puts a clickable link under the Short.
- **Audience:** not made for kids.
- **Altered or synthetic content:** yes.
- **Music credit:** must stay in the description (CC BY licence).
