# Lenny's Scam Lab — channel kit

## Name
**Lenny's Scam Lab**: handle **@LennysScamLab** (free as of 18 Oct 2026; @ScamLab is taken).

Why this name:
- The neon **SCAM LAB** sign is already in every lab shot of every episode, so the name and the picture match.
- "Scam" is the word people search for, and YouTube shows the channel name next to every video.
- "Lenny's" makes it a character channel. People subscribe to a character, not a topic.
- It's short, easy to say out loud, and has no brand or trademark in it.

Backups:
- **Raccon**: raccoon + con. Handle @Raccon is free. Short and clever, but it doesn't say what the channel is about.
- **Trust Me, I Ran This**: the catchphrase. Strong, but too long for a name. Use it as a slogan.
- **Ask Lenny**: simple. Use it later for a Q&A series.

Note: there is also a well-known scam-baiting phone bot called "Lenny" (2011). That's a different niche (pranks). The name won't confuse anyone, and searches for "lenny scam" may even bring some people to us.

## Settings
- **Channel trailer** (for people who aren't subscribed yet): ep01.
- **Featured video** (for subscribers): the latest one.
- **Watermark**: `watermark.png` (Lenny's head), shown "for the entire video".
- **Upload defaults**:
  - category: Education;
  - language: English;
  - default description: the "If you've lost money" block plus the disclaimer.
- **Audience**: "No, it's not made for kids". This is important, otherwise comments and recommendations get switched off.
- **Altered or synthetic content**: tick "Yes" for every video. The images and voice are AI-generated, and YouTube requires creators to disclose this for realistic AI content. It's a cartoon, but disclosing removes any risk.
