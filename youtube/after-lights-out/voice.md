# Voice profile — After Lights Out (faceless narrator, English)

Inferred from Ep.1 "The Group Chat" (the channel's only video so far). Narrator: ElevenLabs "True – Crime & Horror Narrator", eleven_v3.

- **Who we talk to:** one viewer, alone, at night, phone in hand. Always second person: "you", "your door". Never "guys", never "we".
- **How it sounds:** calm, low, unhurried. Short sentences. Facts first, fear second. Whisper on the line that turns the story ([whispers] tags only there).
- **Rhythm:** setup line → one-sentence reaction ("You're 4B. You live alone.") → silence. Quotes are messages from other people, read flat.
- **Words we use:** plain, physical, domestic — door, hallway, couch, peephole, 2:14 a.m., apartment numbers.
- **Words we never use:** "scary", "creepy", "terrifying", "spooky", "insane", "you won't believe", jump-scare noises, gore, demons/ghosts named outright.
- **What we never claim:** that it's a true story, that it happened to a real person or place. All names, numbers and buildings are invented.
- **Ending:** a direct question to the viewer + "Night two is coming."
