## Why this first
- Shorts don't count toward the 4,000 watch hours needed for monetization; long videos do.
- People who watch Nights 1–3 as Shorts get one place to see the whole story, plus a chapter that exists only here.
- Most footage is already built: the three 3D scenes re-render in 16:9 without new modelling.
