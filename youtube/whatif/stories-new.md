# What If At 2:14 — new stories (standalone, not Elm Court)

Owner, Oct 22: keep everything already planned/published (Group Chat Nights 1–3, cuts, long video with Night 4);
new stories are completely different. Same genre: "what if…" horror, second person, everyday tech/places, every story starts at 2:14 a.m.
Format learned from Nights 1–3: 45–60 s, the strange event + question in the first 2 s, no recap, a turn every 5–8 s.
Each story gets 2 nights; a third only if comments ask for "part 2".

| # | Story | Hook (first 2 s) | Night 2 / twist |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The Doorbell Cam** | "Your doorbell camera just recorded someone leaving your house. You live alone." | You scroll back: they walked in three days ago. They never left — until tonight. And the clip ends with them turning to the camera and mouthing your name. |
| 2 | **Missed Call From You** | "At 2:14 a.m. you get a missed call. From your own number." | The voicemail is your voice, whispering: "Don't open the door when I knock." Then the knocking starts. |
| 3 | **Find My** | "Find My says your phone is in the basement. It's in your hand." | The dot moves up the stairs. Stops outside your bedroom. Your phone shows a new device nearby: "Your iPhone". |
| 4 | **The Last Bus** | "The last bus comes at 2:14. Every passenger is asleep. The driver isn't." | Driver: "They all missed their stop. Years ago." The next stop on the screen is your address. |
| 5 | **Room 214** | "The hotel note says: don't open the connecting door. Your room has no connecting door." | Knocks from the wall. In the morning, housekeeping says room 214 has been empty since 1998 — and there's a door in your wall. |
| 6 | **Wrong Number** | "A stranger texts you: 'You forgot to lock the back door.' You don't have a back door." | Next text: a photo of you reading that text, taken from inside your room. |
| 7 | **Night Shift Rules** (gas station) | "Rule 1 of the night shift: if a customer pays at 2:14, don't look at their face." | Rule 4 is crossed out by hand. Someone pays at 2:14 — with your card. |
| 8 | **The Extra Person** | "In every photo from your trip there's one more person. Each one, closer." | The last photo was taken this morning. In your hallway. |

Order: 1 → 2 → 3 (strongest everyday-tech hooks, easy to read on a small screen), then 6, 4, 5, 7, 8.
Rule: every 48 h the story with the most views per subscriber and "part 2" comments gets the next slot.
